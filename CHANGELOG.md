# @omnidotdev/mantle-api

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [#40](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/40) [`3e06bdb`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/3e06bdbb917b2b1c332a80d30775e32b3523ec09) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Plan limits are now enforced for every workspace from its Omni plan: up to the plan's number of contacts (internal team members count too) and invoices created per month (deleting an invoice does not free a slot). A billing outage never blocks reading or editing existing records: the workspace's last known plan applies, and with none known nothing is blocked. Self-hosted deployments without billing configured have no plan limits. A new `workspacePlan` query reports a workspace's plan, limits and usage.

  Security: a signed-in user could read another workspace's record by its id; every lookup is now limited to the caller's workspaces. Workspace settings, document numbering and sales channel connections can only be changed by workspace owners and admins, and stored sales channel credentials are never returned.

- [#41](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/41) [`05e1382`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/05e13827874127018124f30ccdc14d1d902b579c) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Turning a quote into an invoice is now a single `convertQuoteToInvoice` mutation: the invoice, its line items and the quote's converted status are saved together or not at all, a quote can only be invoiced once even from two tabs at the same time, and the conversion counts toward the plan's monthly invoice limit. Deleting an invoice made from a quote no longer fails; the quote goes back to accepted, ready to invoice again.

- [#39](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/39) [`01e88cb`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/01e88cbeeebe8d62af8fffe7c5977444772ae3c3) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Self-hosted instances can sign in through any OpenID Connect provider. With `PERSONAL_WORKSPACE_FALLBACK=true`, a caller whose token carries no workspace claims gets a personal workspace derived from their subject, so subjects that are not UUIDs (emails, `provider|id` pairs) work. Workspace ids now reach the database scope check as a properly quoted list, so an id containing a comma or brace can never grant access to another workspace.

- [#37](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/37) [`3e52a3b`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/3e52a3b674c2973193feadf0692474c1051002b3) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Deleting a workspace now deletes its Mantle data: people, companies, suppliers, products, quotes, invoices with their payments, the credit ledger, document numbering, sales channel connections, and settings

- [#40](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/40) [`3e06bdb`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/3e06bdbb917b2b1c332a80d30775e32b3523ec09) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Workspace settings now store the workspace profile shown on invoices, quotes and contracts (company name, contact, email, phone, website, address, logo and registration state), so it follows the workspace across browsers and devices.

### Patch Changes

- [#43](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/43) [`2179502`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/21795023ee7812c6f1f4679fcceddbb1006e70ee) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Fix invoice, payment, quote, product, company, person, and document numbering changes never publishing their events, so invoices and payments now reach your books in MyFi and products reach Halo's catalog. Invoice events carry the total after recomputation

- [#40](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/40) [`3e06bdb`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/3e06bdbb917b2b1c332a80d30775e32b3523ec09) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Recording a stock adjustment now updates the product's on-hand quantity, and deleting an adjustment reverses it.

- [#41](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/41) [`05e1382`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/05e13827874127018124f30ccdc14d1d902b579c) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Invoice payment links must be a full https link (up to 2048 characters, without a username or password in it); anything else is refused when the invoice is saved, and the printable invoice only ever links to an https address. A workspace's plan is now remembered across restarts, so a restart during a billing outage keeps the workspace on its last known plan instead of lifting its limits.

- [#44](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/44) [`823d09b`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/823d09b1d4a76c3f15d7bd034fdd509b4debc651) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - When a plan's limits can't be read, the free plan now falls back to its advertised limits (100 contacts and 10 invoices a month), and workspace seats are no longer reported as capped, since every plan includes unlimited team members.

- [#38](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/38) [`034de42`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/034de423fcc0561a08fd8dbb5fdb4c9751e765ff) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Move to the `@omnidotdev/providers` v0.2.0 release.

- [#42](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/42) [`ee694c4`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/ee694c479c7e505d40061f1f36bed170065e41f0) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Security: plan limits now hold when many contacts or invoices are created at once, including quote conversions racing with new invoices (parallel requests could previously all get through). A record's workspace can no longer be changed after it is created, and a record can only link to records in its own workspace; the invoice document and quote conversion only use the workspace's own company, contact and products. The credit ledger is append-only: credit is issued with `issueCredit` and applied with `applyInvoiceCredit`, and the generic create, update and delete operations for credit entries are removed. Access tokens can be pinned to this API with the new `AUTH_AUDIENCE` setting (and optionally `AUTH_ISSUER`), so a token issued for another product is refused.

- [#39](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/39) [`01e88cb`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/01e88cbeeebe8d62af8fffe7c5977444772ae3c3) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Each release now publishes a self-host container image to `api.manifold.omni.dev/omnidotdev/mantle-api`, tagged with the version and `latest`.

- [#40](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/40) [`3e06bdb`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/3e06bdbb917b2b1c332a80d30775e32b3523ec09) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Security: the API no longer turns off TLS certificate verification for its outgoing requests. A local development stack with self-signed certificates can opt out with `NODE_TLS_REJECT_UNAUTHORIZED=0`, which production ignores.

- [#36](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/pull/36) [`95a09b3`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/mantle-api/commit/95a09b345af8b854e0a92b05e2b98cc98820971e) Thanks [@coopbri](https://github.com/coopbri)! - Security: every create, update and delete now requires you to belong to the workspace that owns the record (and to any record it links to). Issuing or applying credit and generating document numbers require workspace membership, payments and credit history are visible only to their workspace, and signed-out requests can no longer read records by id.
